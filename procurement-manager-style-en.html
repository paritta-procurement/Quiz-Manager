Self-assessment · Leading procurement teams

What is your management style?

15 scenarios from the day-to-day of leading a procurement team. Answer with what you would actually do — not with what sounds best.

BasisDaniel Goleman’s six leadership styles (HBR, Leadership That Gets Results).
Format15 scenarios, 4 possible reactions each. Pick the one closest to your instinct.
Time5 to 7 minutes. You can go back and adjust answers.
ResultsDominant style, backup style, a profile radar and a reading of your versatility across the six.

There is no "right" style: the research shows the best leaders switch between several, depending on the situation.
